# Banana Photos privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Banana Photos is an offline photo library. It does not collect, store or share any of your data.

- **Your photos stay on your Mac.** The app reads the folders you open, and never uploads, moves or changes your photos unless you ask it to (for example moving photos into Locked Folder, importing or exporting).
- **All analysis is on your device.** Face grouping, pets, documents and search by description run on your Mac with models that ship inside the app. The results are saved only on your Mac.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The app has no servers and sends nothing about you or your use of it anywhere.
- **Locked Folder** is encrypted on your Mac with your passphrase, which is never stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it, the photos in it can't be recovered.
- **Importing from the Photos app** uses Apple's PhotoKit after you allow access, and copies only the items you choose into your library folder.
- **Google Photos import** is optional. If you use it, you sign in to Google in your browser and the app downloads the photos you pick, directly from Google to your Mac. The sign-in token is used only for that import. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
