# Banana Photos privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Banana Photos is an offline photo library for Mac, Windows and Linux, with a phone app. It does not collect, store or share any of your data.

- **Your photos stay on your computer.** The app reads the folders you open, and never uploads, moves or changes your photos unless you ask it to (for example moving photos into Locked Folder, importing, exporting, or moving them to the Trash).
- **All analysis is on your device.** Face grouping, pets, documents, search by description and place names run on your computer with models and data that ship inside the app. The results are saved only on your computer.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The app has no servers and sends nothing about you or your use of it anywhere.
- **Connect phone & computers** is off until you turn it on. Then your phone (with the Banana Photos phone app) or another computer of yours can connect to this computer over your own local network, after you pair it with a code shown on this computer. The connection is encrypted, goes directly between your devices, and never through the internet or any server of ours. What a paired phone sees, backs up or deletes, and what a paired computer syncs (photos, favourites, albums, archive and names you gave people), goes only between your own devices. You can remove a paired device at any time.
- **Locked Folder** is encrypted on your computer with your passphrase, which is never stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it, the photos in it can't be recovered.
- **Importing from the Photos app** (Mac) uses Apple's PhotoKit after you allow access, and copies only the items you choose into your library folder.
- **Google Photos import** is optional. If you use it, you sign in to Google in your browser and the app downloads the photos you pick, directly from Google to your computer. The sign-in token is used only for that import. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
